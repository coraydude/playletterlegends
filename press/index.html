About the game

Short description (50 words)

Letter Legends is a free word game of quick rounds and clever spelling. Build words from seven letter tiles to fill a five row board, outscore four AI legends on the level path and battle friends turn by turn. Collect tile themes, play weekly events and solve a new word puzzle every day.

Long description

Letter Legends is a word game where every letter counts. Each match is five quick rounds: you get seven letter tiles and a board of five rows, from three to seven letters long. Fill a row completely for a Perfect Word. Fill all five and you have built a Legendary Board, worth 100 bonus points.

Rare letters score big, bonus squares double and triple letters and words, and the last three rounds multiply your points up to four times, so one great word late in the match can turn it around. Two free swaps help with a tough rack, and a Gold Tile becomes any letter you need, once per round.

Climb the level path against four AI legends, from friendly Bo to Lex, the boss. Then take on friends or players anywhere in the world in Battles, played turn by turn with no timers, so you move whenever it suits you.

Between matches there is always something to chase: a new Word of the Day puzzle, weekly leagues, rotating events like Treasure Dig, Word Rally and Bonus Bounce, and card packs that unlock collectible tile themes, avatar frames and chat packs. Letter Legends is free to play in ten languages, each with its own dictionary.

Key features

  • Five round matches with a seven tile rack, a five row board and points that multiply in the last three rounds.
  • Perfect Words and Legendary Boards: fill every row for a 100 point bonus.
  • Four AI legends on the level path: Bo, Fen, Vera and Lex.
  • Battles against friends or players worldwide, turn by turn, with no timers.
  • Word of the Day: a new seven letter puzzle every day.
  • Weekly leagues and lightning bolts, from bronze to the star cup.
  • Rotating events: Treasure Dig, Word Rally and Bonus Bounce.
  • Collection: card packs and albums that unlock tile themes, plus avatar frames and chat packs.
  • Ten languages, each with its own word list.
