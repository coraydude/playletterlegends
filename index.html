The word game where every letter counts

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

Letter Legends is a word game of quick rounds and clever spelling. Beat the four legends, battle your friends turn by turn and collect tile themes along the way. Every letter counts.

Spell. Score. Repeat.

Make the best word you can from the letters in your rack. Rare letters score big, and the last three rounds multiply your points up to ×4.

Beat the legends

Climb the level path and face Bo, Fen, Vera and Lex. Bo warms you up. Lex is the boss.

Battle your friends

Challenge a friend or a player anywhere in the world, turn by turn. No timers: take your turn whenever it suits you.

Collect tile themes

Open card packs, fill each tile's four parts and unlock new tile themes. Complete whole albums for bonus rewards.

Events every week

Dig for treasure in Treasure Dig, race in Word Rally and bounce for prizes in Bonus Bounce. A new event is always on its way.
